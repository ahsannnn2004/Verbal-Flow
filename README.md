# Verbal Flow 🌍
**A simple, user-friendly language learning prototype.**

## About This Project
Hi! We built Verbal Flow as our Complex Computing Problem for our Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) course. 

The main goal of this assignment was to find a usability gap in current applications and design a better interface. We realized that a lot of language learning apps fail because they have confusing layouts, too much clutter, or just aren't designed with the user's actual needs in mind. We wanted to fix that and make an app that feels natural to use.

## How We Did It (Our Research)
We didn't just guess what to design. We created a survey and gathered feedback from 50 students to see what they really wanted. 
* We collected **quantitative data** by having them rate the simplicity and navigation of the apps they currently use.
* We collected **qualitative data** by asking them to describe their biggest frustrations, what breaks their focus, and why they might have uninstalled an app in the past.

Based on exactly what those 50 people told us, we designed this high-fidelity prototype in Figma.

## What's Inside (Key Features)
We focused heavily on core HCI principles like simplicity, consistency, and clear feedback. Here is what we built:
* **Conversational Dictionary:** Instead of random vocabulary lists, users learn through practical, real-life scenarios like *Dining Out*, *Airport & Travel*, and *Medical & Health*.
* **AI Chat Tutor:** A built-in chat screen where users can practice real conversations with an AI to build confidence.
* **Clean Lesson Interfaces:** Simple, distraction-free screens for translation and audio exercises that give you instant visual feedback when you get an answer right.

## Tools We Used
* **Figma:** For all the UI/UX design, wireframing, and interactive prototyping.
* **Google Forms:** For conducting our user research survey.
